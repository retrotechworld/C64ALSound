- [ ] 01: triangle A4 is sustained, releases and returns to BASIC.
- [ ] 02: triangle, saw, pulse, noise occur in that order with gaps.
- [ ] 03: three pulse-width sweeps occur without stopping pitch.
- [ ] 04: rising filter sweep becomes audible across the range; no unexpected reset loop.
- [ ] 05: ring-modulated triangle then hard-synced saw, with silent source oscillator.
- [ ] 06: eight lead notes play over two sustained backing notes.
- [ ] Every lesson releases all voices, mutes and returns to BASIC.
- [ ] Repeat on PAL / 6581.
- [ ] Repeat on PAL / 8580, noting filter and waveform differences.
- [ ] Repeat on NTSC; distinguish unadjusted pitch and tempo changes from defects.
- [ ] Retune NTSC frequency tables if matching PAL tuning is required.
- [ ] Repeat on physical hardware, recording board revision and chip marking.
